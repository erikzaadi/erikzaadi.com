## dnd-fam-ftw

### An AI Dungeon Master for family D&D nights

A self-hosted, AI-powered Dungeon Master for short, family-friendly adventures. Built because my kid wanted to play D&D and nobody wanted to run the game. Includes car mode, terminal mode, tiered models and agentic narration.
